Privacy Policy

TurboRAM values your privacy. No tracking, no data collection, no internet connection required.

TurboRAM is provided by Karandeep Singh. For any questions or concerns, please contact me via Email.

TurboRAM does not collect any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device and never connects to the internet. All memory monitoring is done locally and nothing is transmitted or stored outside of your Mac.

This privacy policy may be updated at any time but will never be used to collect more data than is necessary. I believe in transparency and privacy, and this will not change.

Thank you for using TurboRAM.
